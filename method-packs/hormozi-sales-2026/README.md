# Hormozi Sales Operating Method — 2026 Talk Adaptation

This pack converts the in-call and training methods in Alex Hormozi's [The Ultimate Sales Training for 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=StVqS0jD7Ls) into auditable trainer data.

It is an independent adaptation, not an endorsement or a model fine-tune. The source video remains the authority; this pack records timestamped provenance, observable behaviors, counter-evidence, ethical gates, and one-constraint drills. Examples and drill wording are original adaptations rather than copied transcript passages.

Pack contents:

- `manifest.json`: identity, version, source ranges, scope, and ethical boundaries.
- `framework.json`: PPP, BANT, CLOSER, three-pillar presentation, AAA, enterprise adaptation, and observable evidence.
- `rubric.json`: evidence scale, stage weights, confidence rules, and non-negotiable gates.
- `drills.json`: single-constraint deliberate-practice loops.

The app must pin the pack version on every new session and preserve that version in the evaluation. Pack changes require fixture review and a version bump.
